01 — The Philosophy

Rethinking Location Telemetry.

Legacy Models

Many tracking experiences rely on persistent, unrestricted sharing models. Users frequently grant perpetual access once, resulting in continuous background surveillance.

  • × Indefinite Sessions
  • × High Battery Drain
  • × Opaque Background Telemetry

The TrackMate Paradigm

Location data is treated as an explicit, ephemeral, and revocable action. Telemetry is strictly time-bound and remains entirely within the user's control.

  • ✓ Granular Time Windows
  • ✓ Hardware-Level Kill-Switch
  • ✓ Zero Passive Tracking
02 — Security Architecture

Connection ≠ Permission

In TrackMate, being friends or joining a group does not grant location access. The social layer is structurally isolated from the telemetry layer.
